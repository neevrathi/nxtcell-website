Alcatel, powered by NXTCell

Global heritage, engineered for India

Alcatel is a globally recognised mobile brand with a rich French heritage, celebrated for delivering innovative yet accessible technology across the world. Relaunched in India by NXTCell, it offers a complete mobile ecosystem — from feature-rich smartphones and powerful tablets to durable feature phones. The portfolio includes TCL smartphones and tablets as well.

Combining global design, advanced engineering and local relevance, the line-up blends style, performance and value — making premium experiences more accessible to every Indian household.
