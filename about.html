Who we are

A globally trusted brand, driving its B2C journey in India

NXTCell India holds the exclusive licence for the Alcatel B2C and TCL consumer technology segments. We are driving that journey in India with a bold ambition: to take Indian-grown consumer electronics to the world.

We aim to become a hub for launching global brands and introducing new product categories across varied price points, tailored to India’s evolving digital-first consumer base. With a robust retail and distribution ecosystem, we are committed to building sustainable growth for our partners while offering world-class devices that seamlessly blend design, performance and usability.

Our vision

To build India’s most admired tech ecosystem — creating globally respected brands that are made in India, built for the world.

Our mission

To design and deliver innovative, high-quality consumer tech across categories, combining global insight with local excellence — through world-class partnerships, Indian manufacturing and a brand-first approach.

Our foundations

Manufacturing excellence, built on partnership
