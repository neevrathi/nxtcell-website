Who we are

A global licence, and a brand of our own

NXTCell holds a global brand licence to sell Alcatel, and is currently building its own homegrown brand, NX. Together they let us serve every price point — a globally trusted name with French heritage, alongside a connected ecosystem designed here from first principles.

We aim to become a hub for launching global brands and introducing new product categories across varied price points, tailored to India’s evolving digital-first consumer base. With a robust retail and distribution ecosystem, we are committed to building sustainable growth for our partners while offering world-class devices that blend design, performance and usability.

NXTCell is building next-generation brands, manufactured in India, catering to the world.

Our vision

To build India’s most admired tech ecosystem — creating globally respected brands that are made in India, built for the world.

Our mission

To design and deliver innovative, high-quality consumer tech across categories, combining global insight with local excellence — through world-class partnerships, Indian manufacturing and a brand-first approach.

Our foundations

Manufacturing excellence, built on partnership

Leadership

At the helm
