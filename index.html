Who we are

A hub for launching global brands in India

Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Faridabad, NXTCell holds the exclusive licence for the Alcatel B2C and TCL consumer technology segments in India.

We aim to become a hub for launching global brands and introducing new product categories across varied price points, tailored to India’s evolving digital-first consumer base. With a robust retail and distribution ecosystem, we are committed to building sustainable growth for our partners while offering devices that blend design, performance and usability.

Technology

NXTPAPER: a display built for eye comfort

NXTPAPER is a display technology designed to give a paper-like visual experience — reducing eye strain while still delivering vivid, crisp visuals, even in bright daylight. It sits at the heart of our V3 Series and future launches.
