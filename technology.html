Display

NXTPAPER display technology

NXTPAPER is a display technology designed to provide a paper-like visual experience — reducing eye strain while delivering vivid, crisp visuals, even in bright daylight. Developed with our global ODM partner, it is built for eye comfort, low blue light and paper-like viewing.

It sits at the heart of our V3 Series and future launches. Whether it is students, professionals or entertainment seekers, NXTPAPER changes the way India engages with screens.
